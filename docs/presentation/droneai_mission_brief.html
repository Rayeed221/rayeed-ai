01 · Executive summary

A software-defined drone that understands spoken orders

RayeedAI turns a commercial ArduPilot airframe into an assistant you talk to. The AI handles the routine flying. A rule-based safety core can overrule the AI at any time. For any action that delivers a payload, a human makes the final decision.

02 · The operational problem

Drones help in a crisis, but flying them takes the operator's hands and eyes

Cognitive overload

A section commander or fire officer can't run a joystick, a map and a radio at the same time. Today's UAS need a dedicated, trained pilot.

Language barrier

Ground-control software is English-first and menu-driven. Front-line users in Bangladesh think and speak in Bangla, especially under stress.

GPS-denied, cluttered spaces

Jamming, urban canyons, smoke and indoor fires break GPS-only autonomy. The drone has to see and map its surroundings on its own.

Army need

Persistent eyes over a perimeter or convoy, fast reconnaissance of a compound, and a precise, accountable payload capability that keeps soldiers out of direct exposure.

Fire-service need

Reach upper floors that ladders can't reach in time, find the seat of the fire through smoke, and knock down early-stage fires before crews arrive.

Section summary

Both users need the same thing: a drone they can direct by voice, that flies itself safely in difficult environments, and that delivers a payload only when a human decides it should.

    Section summary

    The safety checks are deterministic code, not AI. If the AI asks to fly into an obstacle, climb past the ceiling, or keep going on a dying battery, the request is blocked. Repeated failures escalate to abort or failsafe. The same gate structure carries over to payload release for both variants.

    07 · Perception & onboard autonomy

    Sees, maps and reasons on the edge

    Built

    DroNet avoidance

    PULP-DroNet v3 CNN on the Myriad X VPU at ~20 Hz. Outputs collision probability and steering, and sends velocity corrections directly over MAVLink.

    Built

    VIO + SLAM

    RTAB-Map visual-inertial odometry plus SLAM gives a 6-DOF pose without GPS, a 5 cm 3D occupancy grid, and relocalization into saved maps.

    Built

    Object detection

    On-device YOLOv6n with stereo depth returns objects with 3D coordinates (mm), converted to NED/GPS through the localizer.

    Built

    Thinking oracle

    A small local model (qwen3 0.6B via Ollama) reviews each critical step. If it fails, the system falls back to deterministic rules, so it works with no cloud link.

    What this means for Defense

    Navigation still works when GPS is jammed or spoofed (VIO pose plus a saved map). Detections come with geo-coordinates for reporting, and the drone avoids obstacles on its own in urban terrain and under tree cover.

    What this means for Fire

    Indoor and stairwell flight without GPS, and a 3D map of the building to brief crews. Thermal fusion is on the roadmap because stereo depth weakens in dense smoke.

    Section summary

    All perception runs on a palm-sized camera with its own AI chip, with no ground server needed. The drone keeps flying and avoiding obstacles even if the voice link or the cloud LLM drops.

    08 · Variant A — Defense

    Army configuration: a squad-level aerial asset

    The same flight brain with a ruggedized airframe, an EO/IR gimbal and a modular payload bay. The commander talks to it like a radio operator.

    ISR & overwatch

    "Hold 80 m above checkpoint 2 and report movement." Uses goto_position → HOVER → vision_detect_objects with geo-tagged detections.

    Perimeter patrol

    Waypoint loops around a base or forward post, with automatic return-to-launch on low battery or link loss (the existing RTL and failsafe workflows).

    Convoy route scouting

    Flies ahead of a convoy, flags obstructions and possible ambush points, and reports distance and bearing (get_distance_to_str).

    Building & compound recon

    GPS-denied entry using VIO+SLAM, producing a 3D map of rooms and corridors before troops enter.

    Target marking & reporting

    Locates and geo-marks a point of interest for a human-directed fires or command chain. The drone reports; people decide.

    Mission payload

    A modular bay for supply drops, smoke and marker canisters, or an approved mission payload, released only through the authorization chain on the next slide.

    Section summary

    Most of the Army value is in seeing and reporting, and the software can already do that. The payload capability adds a module on top of the existing pipeline, under strict human authority.

    Design rules

    • payload_* tools are permission level critical
    • The voice LLM can't arm or release a payload
    • Authorization expires after a set time window

    Physical safety

    • A separate hardware master-arm, independent of the AI computer
    • Payload goes safe automatically on FAILSAFE, RTL or link loss
    • Geofence and no-release zones

    Accountability

    • Every request, authorization and actor is logged with a timestamp
    • Telemetry and video kept for after-action review
    • Aligned with IHL and Rules of Engagement

    10 · Variant B — Firefighting

    Fire-service configuration: first on scene, from the air

    A heat-tolerant airframe with a thermal camera and an extinguisher payload. The station officer dispatches it by voice while crews are still on the way.

    High-rise fire attack

    Flies to the reported floor, finds the hottest window by thermal imaging, holds a standoff distance, and delivers extinguisher balls through the opening.

    Early knock-down

    Drops dry-chemical or AFO extinguisher units on early-stage fires in markets, garment factories and warehouses before they spread.

    Wildfire & hill-forest spotting

    Patrols for hotspots, geo-tags fire fronts, and reports spread direction to incident command.

    Interior scouting

    Smoke-filled interiors without GPS: SLAM builds a map of stairwells and rooms, and YOLO flags people for rescue teams.

    Hazmat & industrial

    Inspects chemical, gas or electrical fires from a safe distance before crews go in (existing inspection workflow).

    Incident command view

    A live overhead view with position and telemetry for the commander, plus automatic return-to-launch on low battery.

    Section summary

    The firefighting variant reuses the same voice, safety and navigation stack. What's new is thermal targeting and an extinguisher payload that flies straight into the gaps ladders and hoses can't reach quickly.

    11 · Variant B — Suppression workflow (interactive)

    From "fire on the 9th floor" to extinguisher delivered

    Step through a suppression sortie. The operator confirms every drop, which is faster than the Defense chain but still a human decision.

    Extinguisher payloads

    • AFO-type self-activating balls (~1.3 kg each)
    • Dry-chemical canister with a burst charge
    • Option: tethered hose for sustained spray

    Heat & smoke safety

    • Standoff distance enforced by SLAM proximity (≥1.5 m)
    • Thermal ceiling gate on approach (roadmap)
    • Automatic RTL if updrafts push altitude or speed past the limits

    Drop accuracy

    • Hover hold from VIO pose plus GPS
    • Release point accounts for height and drift
    • Re-check with thermal after each drop
    Section summary

    A fire sortie is a short chain: detect, approach, aim, confirm, drop, reassess. The safety core keeps the drone out of heat and away from walls, and a single operator confirmation releases each extinguisher.

    15 · Roadmap

    From lab to field

    PHASE 1
    done

    Autonomy core

    Voice → tools → safety → state machine, simulator and MAVLink adapters, DroNet and VIO/SLAM integration, 235 tests.

    PHASE 2
    0–3 months

    Flight validation

    SITL-to-real flights on the RPi 5 build, a waypoint ISR demo, GPS-denied VIO hover tests, and tuning of the avoidance gains.

    PHASE 3
    3–6 months

    Payload & thermal modules

    payload_* tools with the authorization chain and audit log, a hardware master-arm, thermal hotspot detection, and extinguisher drop trials on controlled fires.

    PHASE 4
    6–12 months

    Variant prototypes

    Defense: ruggedized airframe, EO/IR gimbal, secure link, trials with an Army unit. Fire: heat-shielded airframe, trials with the fire service.

    PHASE 5
    12+ months

    Certification & production

    Regulatory approval, operator training syllabus in Bangla, and a small-batch production line.

    Section summary

    The plan runs five phases over about 12 months. The core is finished. The next steps are real-flight validation, then payload and thermal modules, then variant-specific prototypes trialled with real end users.
